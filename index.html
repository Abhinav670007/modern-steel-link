Capabilities

Plate to Assembly, in Four Passes

Every job moves through the same four stations, in-house from first cut to final coat.

  1. 01

    Cut

    Shearing & Punching

    Sheet stock is squared on the guillotine shear and pierced on flywheel power presses to the exact blank size.

  2. 02

    Form

    Press-Brake Bending

    A hydraulic press brake folds each blank into panels, doors and frames to size.

  3. 03

    Weld

    Welding & Fabrication

    Panels are welded and framed into complete cabinet and enclosure bodies.

  4. 04

    Finish

    Fitting & Finishing

    Hinges, locks and shelving are fitted, then the unit is primed, painted and prepped for delivery.

Who We Are

About Us

Modern Steel Link Industry designs and fabricates custom steel cabinets, lockers and enclosures built for daily use — not just spec-sheet numbers. Every job runs through our own shop, start to finish, so the people who cut your sheet are the same people who stand behind the weld.

Our Mission

To be the preferred partner for high-quality, custom-engineered steel cabinets and enclosures — exceeding expectations through precise manufacturing, honest lead times and dependable service.

To keep improving our process and our equipment as fabrication technology moves forward.

Our Vision

To be the long-term partner clients call before the drawings are even final — because the last job held up.

To build a shop that's good for our crew, our clients, and the community around us.
